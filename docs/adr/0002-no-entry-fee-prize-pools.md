# No Entry Fees and no Prize Pools

The product was charted as paid Leagues: Members pay an Entry Fee in Telegram Stars, and the winner takes the pooled fees. We dropped that entirely. Joining a League is always free, payments never fund a reward, and the app takes no commission because there is nothing to take it from. Three independent blockers ruled it out (research for #2–#4):

- **Technical:** no Bot API method pays Stars to a user. Only full refunds, Telegram gifts/Premium, or developer withdrawal to TON via Fragment exist.
- **Platform policy:** Stars ToS §4/§6.1, Bot Developer ToS §5.2(h), Apple 5.3.1/5.3.3 and Google Play's real-money contest policy all bar a prize funded by paid entries.
- **Law:** a paid pooled prediction prize is pool betting or gambling in the UK, Germany, France, Russia and the US, and the app is its organiser even among friends and even with zero commission.

Money comes instead from Paid features (never affecting points, standings or Prizes) and Tips. Winners get a Trophy with no monetary value. Real Prizes exist only in developer-run Official Leagues: free entry, fixed in-kind Prize announced in advance, never in private Leagues (otherwise one person with two accounts could farm Prizes). The app also does not help Members settle side bets among themselves.

## Considered Options

- **Stars pool paid out via owner withdrawal to TON:** off-platform payout is still unlicensed real-money gaming and breaks Apple 5.3.3 at the moment of payment.
- **Pool paid as Telegram gifts/Premium:** still a prize funded by entries, so still pool betting and against store policy.
- **TON-priced Leagues with a gambling licence:** legally possible but turns the project into a regulated gambling business (KYC/AML, geo-blocking, taxes). Rejected for scope.
