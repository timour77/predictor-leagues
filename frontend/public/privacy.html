Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]. This explains what Predictor Leagues (the "app", run by an independent developer, "we") stores about you. Contact: [CONTACT].

What we receive from Telegram

When you open the app, Telegram tells us your Telegram user id, first name, username (if you have one) and profile photo link. We use the id to recognise you and the rest to show you to other Members of your Leagues.

What we store

  • Profile: Telegram id, first name, username, photo link, and when your account was created.
  • Leagues: Leagues you created or joined, and your Predictions (the scores you guessed).
  • Results: final standings and Trophies from Leagues you played.
  • Messages: short notices the bot sends you (for example when a League finishes), kept until delivered.
  • Tips: if you tip, the amount, time, and the Telegram payment reference. Payments are processed by Telegram; we never receive card or other payment details.

We do not collect your phone number, location or contacts, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who sees it

Other Members of a League see your name, photo, standings and, after each match kicks off, your Prediction for it. Nobody else sees your Predictions. We do not sell or share your data. It is stored with our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Neon), who process it on our behalf, and the match data provider receives nothing about you.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use the app. Payment records are kept as long as needed to handle refunds and disputes and to meet legal obligations.

Deleting your data

Write to [CONTACT] from your Telegram account and ask us to delete your data. We will erase your name, username, photo, Predictions and pending bot messages, and detach your Telegram id from the account. What remains is anonymous: that a "Deleted user" took part in a League (so other Members' standings do not change) and the payment records of any Tips.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at [CONTACT].

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and every request is verified with Telegram's signed login data. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep secrets out of the code and limit who can access the database.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
