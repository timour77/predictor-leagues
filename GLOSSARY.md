# Glossary

**League** — a private competition among friends, created by one user, joined by invite (the developer-run exception is an Official League). Members compete by predicting match results. Not to be confused with a football league (see Competition).

**Competition** — a real-world football tournament or championship (Premier League, Champions League, World Cup). A League tracks one or more Competitions.

**Match** — one real-world fixture of a Competition, with its kickoff time, status and final score. Members predict Matches; the app keeps its own copy of each Match, taken from the external match data source.

**Member** — a user who has joined a League. Joining is always free.

**Creator** — the Member who created the League.

**Trophy** — a permanent mark on a user's profile for winning a League. It has no monetary value and cannot be transferred.

**Official League** — a League run by the developer rather than by friends, free to join, which may carry a Prize. Private Leagues never carry a Prize.

**Prize** — a fixed in-kind reward (e.g. Telegram Premium or a gift) that the developer funds and announces before an Official League starts. It never depends on how many users joined or what anyone paid.

**Paid feature** — something a user buys with Telegram Stars that improves the service (e.g. more Leagues, extra statistics, cosmetics). It never affects points, standings or the chance of a Prize.

**Tip** — Telegram Stars a user gives the developer voluntarily, getting nothing in return that affects play.

_Avoid_: Entry Fee, Prize Pool, stake, bet — nobody pays to join a League, and payments never fund a reward.

**Prediction** — a Member's forecast for one match within one League: a single exact score. The outcome (home win / draw / away win) follows from the score, so the two can never disagree. Each League has its own Predictions; the same user may predict the same match differently in different Leagues. A match with no Prediction by its Deadline scores 0 points for that Member (a Missed match) and cannot count towards the exact-score tiebreak.

**Deadline** — the moment a match kicks off. A Prediction can be made only before it; each match has its own Deadline, and a rescheduled match carries its Deadline with it. Until then a Prediction can be changed or withdrawn freely; the last version stands.

**Reveal** — the moment the other Members' Predictions for a match become visible: its Deadline. Before that, a Member sees only their own Predictions and whether each other Member has made one (not the score).

**Dropped match** — a match that scores nothing for anyone: cancelled, abandoned and not completed, or not played within the grace period after the Window ends. Predictions on it are ignored. A match awarded by a governing body (e.g. a 3-0 walkover) is not dropped; it scores by the awarded result.

**Grace period** — 7 days after the Window ends during which postponed or interrupted matches may still be played and counted. Settlement waits for it; matches still unplayed afterwards become Dropped matches.

**Window** — the date range of a League. Only matches of the League's Competitions inside the Window count. Joining closes when the first match of the Window kicks off.

**Settlement** — closing a League once every match in its Window is finished or dropped: final standings are fixed and the winner receives a Trophy. Ties on points are broken by number of exact scores; if still tied, all tied Members are winners and each receives a Trophy.

**Cancellation** — a League that has fewer than two Members when its Window starts is cancelled and its Creator is notified.
